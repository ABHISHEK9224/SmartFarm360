ABOUT SMARTFARM360

Smarter Decisions for Better Farming.

SmartFarm360 is an integrated agricultural decision-support platform designed to help farmers make better decisions by considering multiple farm conditions together.

🌾

What is SmartFarm360?

SmartFarm360 brings important farming decisions into one platform. Instead of considering only one factor, the system combines land, soil, water, weather, season and budget information.

The platform is designed to support crop selection, resource planning, economic estimation and agricultural risk assessment.
